The Problem
Fragmented Reading Journeys
Existing platforms made discovery, reading, and community interaction feel disjointed. Users struggled to find new stories matching their taste without friction-heavy flows.
✨
The Solution
Unified Reading Ecosystem
NovelNest brings discovery, immersive reading, publishing tools, and community into a seamless mobile-first experience with an expressive dark visual language.
🛠️
My Role
End-to-End UI/UX Designer
Conducted user research, defined personas, created information architecture, and designed all 16 hi-fi screens across 2 user flows — reader and author.
